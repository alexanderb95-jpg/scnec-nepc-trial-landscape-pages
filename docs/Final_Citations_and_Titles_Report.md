### How to Use This Document

2. **For Reference Slide:** Create a final "References" slide at the end of your presentation listing all citations [1] through [10] in the format provided above.

3. **For Titles:** Update slide titles according to the suggestions. Suggested titles are generally more concise and professional while maintaining the same meaning.
