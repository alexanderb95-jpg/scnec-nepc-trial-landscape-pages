## Key Points from Analysis to Consider:

From the Question2_Lydia_Cystectomy_Threshold analysis:
- **Primary finding:** Pre-cystectomy ctDNA is significantly associated with post-cystectomy MRD
- **Threshold:** 0.1 MTM/mL (bootstrap-validated)
- **Performance:** Sensitivity ~82%, Specificity ~68%, PPV ~60%, NPV ~86%
- **Association:** OR 1.89 per 10-fold increase (95% CI 1.37–2.76), p<0.001
- **Clinical role:** Risk stratification for anticipating post-operative MRD
- **Implication:** Identifies patients who may warrant adjuvant therapy considerations

Note: The threshold serves for **risk stratification**, not as a decision tool for surgery vs. additional therapy. It helps identify patients at higher risk of post-operative ctDNA positivity, which may inform monitoring intensity and adjuvant therapy considerations.
